The floor presses against Yun's legs as the hesitation in her qi continues. Cold reaches through her clothing. She feels each heartbeat pass through a body that can, for these few moments, change with time.

Time reaches her fully. She has watched it pass through everything around her, herself unchanged. Now another beat brings another moment in which something can happen to her.

Her weight rests firmly on the ground.

She attends to the stone under her, the cold against her skin, and the body sitting in the mountain. She is here. The pressure of the floor gives that knowledge a particular shape.

The small cave fire jumps. Orange light moves over the walls as a flame rises, briefly interrupting the steady crackle.

A dry leaf rests on the gray fabric across her lap. Wind may have carried it through the cave mouth. Its curled edge lies nearest the fire, close enough for the heat to catch it. The leaf begins to burn.

Black spreads into the cloth under the leaf. She looks at the scorched weave. The mark is small, definite, and visible against the gray.

Under the cloth, a sting reaches her skin. She lifts the damaged fabric and finds a red dot no larger than a millet grain. Heat has passed through to her thigh.

She stares at it.

Normally her qi would shield her. It has done so for millennia, without requiring her attention. Such a small flame would not even register through the protection. She has grown accustomed to ordinary heat as harmless to her flesh as to stone.

The red point is still there. She looks from it to the scorched cloth, tracing the path of the heat. For one beat of her heart, her protection has lapsed enough for the burn to reach her.

She counts.

One. Two. Three.

She has counted before, waiting through danger or attending to another person's life. Now each count belongs to her own mortality. Another small injury could reach her during this interval. She feels the weight of each number as it passes.

Four. Five.

The red dot is still there, definite, the evidence.

Her breath catches around a single word.

Finally.

She has reached what she has tried to describe for so long, and it is small enough to cover with a fingertip. She keeps her hand away and wants only to see the mark. The word holds while she watches, plain, the smallest part of what she has wanted.

Six. Seven.

The heartbeat passes. The moment closes.

Qi returns at the edge of the hesitation. Its pressure gathers while the red dot is still visible. For seven heartbeats, seven intervals she has counted herself, time has passed through a body vulnerable to this small burn. She has not commanded the mortality into being. It has happened while she sat here.

The water drips. The fire crackles. The stone is cold.

Already seven heartbeats of mortality in a life measured in millennia.

She watches the red dot without blinking. Water drips behind her; she hardly hears it. After so much waiting, she has something she can see on her own skin. She won't spend a moment looking elsewhere.

The red dot. Red on skin. Proof.
