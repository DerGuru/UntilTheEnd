Her fingers curl against her palms as she watches the red mark. Nails press crescents into the skin. Her jaw locks; her teeth meet with a click that echoes through the cave.

Qi slams back through her.

The opening closes under the pressure. The brief hesitation in her flesh ends. Renewal returns more forcefully than before, tightening through the places where it had eased. The certainty that has maintained her for millennia fills those small spaces before she can let them stand.

The red dot heals.

She watches it. Red becomes pink. Pink becomes skin. The mark is gone.

Seven heartbeats of evidence disappear in two. She looks at the place where the burn was, memorizing its position against the unmarked skin. The same reflex that preserved her has erased what she finally managed to experience.

She breathes heavily. Air enters in great gulps, lifting her chest beyond comfort. She cannot yet slow the next breath, though she sits beside the same small fire. Her body is responding to a danger she had wanted to permit.

Sweat forms on her forehead. Her palms are damp around the crescents left by her nails. Even those shallow marks begin to heal while she tries to ease her fingers open. Fear lingers in the pressure of her grip after the injury that provoked it is gone.

Her pulse drums in her ears, each beat louder than the dripping water, each one knocking against her chest as she struggles for a slower breath. The sound fills the space where she had counted.

The fire burns at the same height. Water falls in the same place at the back of the cave. She listens to those steady sounds as she shakes, waiting for the rush inside her to ease.

The survival reflex.

It reaches back past thought or language, into the need to continue. She knows it through the tightening under her skin. For millennia every threat to that continuation has met the same response. It has built the resistance she keeps encountering, repeating an old belief until the belief has become as solid as her own existence.

One finger straightens, then another. She eases the last fingertips away from her palms, releasing muscles that have been holding as tightly as they can. The skin is smooth now. Qi has repaired the marks before she has finished releasing the pressure that made them.

The seven heartbeats are already distant, hard to place beside the smooth skin before her. She tries to recall each count. She sat here through it, by this fire, watching the mark herself. The memory belongs to her as surely as the hands resting on her knees.

Even the fabric is smooth again. She touches the gray cloth where the leaf burned it, finding the weave intact.

But she knows.

She knows it was there. She counted seven heartbeats in which heat could injure her. She holds to that plain account while her breathing eases.

The water drips. The cave echoes. The fire crackles.

The fear lingers as her pulse slows. So does the certainty under it. A child in a cave had needed to survive. That need holds deeper than the understanding she has brought to it, stronger than the intention with which she began.

Her hands tremble. She watches them tremble. She lets them.

Outside the cave, the wind changes direction. A different whistle passes through the entrance, and the fire leans toward the cave mouth.

She is still sitting.

She is still here.

The burn mark is gone.

But it was real.
