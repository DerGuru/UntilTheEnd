Yun enters a cave whose walls run with condensation. It is darker than the place she remembers, colder and wetter under her hand. No turn in the passage is familiar. Water drips somewhere deeper, striking stone worn smooth by the drops. She listens, then chooses a place near the entrance.

She builds a small fire by hand. A spark catches in the tinder; she bends close to breathe it into flame. The process is old enough that her hands know every movement. Orange light fills the cave as the fire takes, warming the stone in front of her. It is the color of nights when light marked the space where she could rest safely.

She sits.

Cold seeps through her clothing where she sits cross-legged. She registers it without directing qi toward the discomfort. The stone presses against her legs. She feels exactly where she touches the floor and lets that small demand on her attention stand.

The fire crackles between the sounds of dripping water. Wind pushes through the cave mouth. Flames lean in the gust, then recover.

She breathes.

Air enters her lungs, cool against her throat. Her ribs rise, then settle as the breath leaves. Another breath follows without being arranged. She attends to the rhythm her body has maintained through so much.

She does not push.

The pushing failed. She remembers the mornings in the clearing clearly enough to leave that method alone. Each time she pressed harder, the resistance became more solid. Her declaration of mortality had reinforced the certainty that rejected it.

Here she sits through another breath without making a declaration.

The first surge of flame settles into a steady burn. The crackling quiets, the light holds, and warmth reaches her face evenly.

The water drips. The wind gusts. The stone is cold.

Something shifts inside her.

She follows the faint sensation under the familiar flow of qi. It reaches deeper than the effort she has been making, into the part of herself she usually notices only through its resistance.

The force that has held her unchanged for millennia eases almost imperceptibly.

It is like a whisper, without the sound. A small loosening precedes any movement she can name. She stays with it as space opens in something rigid for so long that she has taken its shape for her own.

Her qi continues to soften.

Then the constant renewal in her flesh hesitates. She feels the pause where the body has always continued smoothly, preserving itself without a seam between one moment and the next.

She leaves the sensation alone. It has come without an instruction she can repeat. For now she can only sit through the unfamiliar interval in a body that has always maintained itself so reliably.

The fire crackles. A piece of wood shifts. Sparks rise.

She sits very still, unwilling to disturb the change by moving or by reaching toward it too intently. She has turned too many small openings into another exercise.

The hesitation continues through one heartbeat, then a second. Long enough for her to tell an event from a fleeting impression.

Her body holds in that unfamiliar interval.

She leaves her hands where they rest. She breathes at the same depth as before, letting the faint opening be, demanding nothing from it.

The water drips. The wind gusts. The fire burns.

The hesitation holds.
