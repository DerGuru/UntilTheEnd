Yun sits on the rock below the empty ridge. For a while she keeps looking toward the place where Zhìwēng stood. Then her eyes settle on the slope before her.

The sentence stays with her, close to the tightness below her sternum. Don't be afraid.

She breathes the same thin, cold air as during the conversation. It carries the mineral sharpness of stone with a hint of recent rain. Her chest expands more easily now. She has begun leaving the next breath alone, letting it come without choosing its depth.

Xiǎohú sits next to her, cleaning each paw in turn. Birds call again from the scrub below, their notes carrying through the still spaces between gusts.

She speaks.

She sends her voice past Xiǎohú toward the open slope. No one is there to receive the words. She needs to hear them aloud.

"I have lived. That was enough."

Her voice is quiet but audible above the wind. She is testing the sentence in air, hearing it as someone else might hear it. It is the truth she recognized in Wei's life, spoken now about her own.

The words sound hollow.

There is little certainty under them. She understands the sentence; she has even seen how it could be true. Yet it feels borrowed. She can wear the thought without making it hers.

Wei's truth fits her imperfectly. She sits with the difference after her voice stops.

Hollow things can be filled. A sentence repeated through experience may acquire the truth it lacks. The distance holds a possibility: something she may grow into rather than force into place.

She has said it. Out loud. The air is fresh. The cold is real.

Her next breath comes while she is still turning the sentence over. She notices it only as the air leaves. Her body has carried on without her, settling into a rhythm she allows.

Below the rock, the mountain descends. A sparse sequence of footholds leads through the scrub and stone toward a distant treeline. Farther down, something interrupts the forest's colors. She squints into the flat light.

The terrain flattens into a valley. Green lies in ordered patches there, distinct from the irregular growth above. Fields. Someone has cleared that ground, tended it, and planted what will feed them. She follows the cultivated edges as far as she can see.

Xiǎohú follows her gaze down the slope, ears turned toward the valley.

Yun stands with the sentence still close to her breath. The mountain falls away below her. In the valley, people live on the ground she is looking at.

She has said it. The words are empty. They will fill.

Or they won't.

She allows either. After so many solitary attempts, she has reached the edge of what she can learn by sitting with herself. She looks toward the fields, a place where the next part might happen among other people.

She takes a step. Down. Toward the treeline. Toward the valley. Toward the smoke.

The step is heavy. Stone presses against her sole as she shifts her weight down the slope. She accepts the effort of it.

One step. Then another. The descent begins.

Above the peak, the clouds begin to dissolve.
