She lies on the roof while constellations shift west and new ones rise in the east. The tiles have cooled under her back.

The fear is still here.

She had expected the decision to change something. Understanding should have dissolved the weight, or accepting the feeling should have transformed it. Yet the same pressure sits under her sternum. She had expected so much in return for allowing it.

Good.

She would have distrusted an immediate disappearance. Understanding alone would have ended this long ago if it were sufficient. Instead it gave her permission to stop fighting.

So she stops.

She lets the feeling be without arguing with it. It is as plain as the cooled tiles. She has spent so much effort demanding that it change. Now she leaves that demand unmade. The next breath comes at its usual depth. There is no achievement to measure it against.

The fear is here. She is here. The night is here.

Nothing changes.

The old conviction is still there. Not now, the refusal that became immortality. Its shape is plain, but she gives it no further support. A fire burns on after the last fuel is added. She lies beside this one and does not feed it.

She lies on the roof and does not defy.

The difference is small enough that a watcher would see nothing happen. She has repeated the refusal every day since that frightened beginning. Here is a moment in which she leaves it unrenewed. Then another follows.

Stillness spreads through the effort she has stopped making. The fear, the years she spent resisting it, and the quiet left by allowing both are all there. She can let go of the struggle without denying any part of it.

The stars turn. The village is quiet below. Xiǎohú is in the house, a small warmth Yun knows.

Nothing else happens. She can let that be enough. An effort sustained for so long had become invisible to her. Its ending leaves a space she can inhabit without filling it at once. The stars move at their slow pace. She has spent so many mornings seeking a visible result that allowing this quiet takes longer than she expected.

Night deepens toward the watch between late and early, when the village is quietest. Even the insects fall still.

She breathes with the fear in her chest. They have shared that space for so long. Now she acknowledges the feeling without letting it decide what she does next. She leaves it where it is.

Quietly, the fear loosens.

It thins like fog in warming air. She can locate it still, but it takes less of her attention. The pressure eases until she remembers its earlier weight more clearly than she feels it.

Her chest is lighter against the night air. She can remember what she has carried without carrying all of it through this breath.

She rests with her hands open under the stars.

Light.

She climbs down from the roof.

Bare feet on the ladder rungs.

The grass.

Dew wets her feet. Each blade presses against her soles, and small drops break between her toes. She has walked on grass for millennia. She cannot remember when it last felt this clear and leaves the question alone.

She stands in the wet dark and breathes.

Her breath reaches its full depth. It leaves easily, past the place where she usually holds it a beat too long. Another comes, then another. The ordinary rhythm asks nothing of her.

Earth, dew, and the green scent of plants at night reach her separately. She has stopped bracing against that clarity.

Grass presses under each step to the door. Her hand finds the wooden frame, then the iron handle. The door opens inward onto the dark house.

Xiǎohú lifts her head from the bed. Amber eyes turn toward Yun, then narrow again as the fox lowers her head.

She lies down. The mattress is hard. The pillow is thin. The blanket is adequate.

She closes her eyes.

She sleeps without dreams. The effort she maintained for millennia has eased enough for rest. The hard mattress holds her as she settles. The blanket lies loose over her shoulders. Her breathing runs without the small vigilance that used to accompany even her quietest moments.

Xiǎohú settles at the foot of the bed. Outside the window, the village is quiet under the stars.

Outside, the first light touches the ridge.
