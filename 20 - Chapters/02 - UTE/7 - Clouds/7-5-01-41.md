Yun climbs the steep gray path, one foot above the other on the stone. Her knees take the incline. Her ankles adjust where the ground tilts, carrying her through movements she has repeated for longer than she can recall.

Heavy clouds lie over the ridgeline. Their flat underside does not move. They look solid enough to rest on the mountain.

She is alone.

The silence is bottomless. When they walked together, another person's breathing ran under it. Footsteps kept pace with hers. Even when neither spoke, speech was available. Now a thought passes without anyone close enough to hear it. She listens to her own steps and finds nothing under them but the mountain.

Xiǎohú moves ahead, rust-colored against the gray stone. She places her paws easily on the broken surface. Her bright eyes catch the light when she turns, a brief warmth of color on the colorless trail.

Wind.

The wind is cold enough to reach her fingers. Usually she registers the temperature from beyond the protection of her qi. Now the cold lies against her skin, a sharp difference that runs to the fingertips.

She stops and raises her hands, turning them toward the light. The change contradicts what her qi has done for longer than the mountains around her have stood. Nothing else holds her attention but the tips of her fingers.

They are blue. Blood has drawn away from the cold extremities, conserving warmth deeper in her body. She knows that response in others. Her own body has not needed it in millennia. She looks from one hand to the other. The same pale blue shows in both.

For one breath, one interval she can count, her qi has left her unshielded.

For one breath, she is exposed.

She exhales onto her hands. Warm air from her own lungs meets the cold skin. She rubs her palms together, then works along the fingers. Friction warms the places where one hand passes over the other. She feels exactly where that warmth comes from.

She rubs her hands together like a human.

She looks at the blue fingertips. This is what she has been trying to allow. Yet she draws another breath to warm them, unwilling to let the cold stay. Both impulses fill the same moment: wonder at what has reached her, fear of being exposed to it.

The blue retreats as qi returns through her fingers. Warmth spreads past the places she has rubbed. The protection settles around her hands, restoring their color. The wind moves over her skin and no longer reaches it.

But the blue was there.

She stops rubbing to look again. Her fingertips have their ordinary color, the same unmarked skin she has known for millennia. She turns a finger toward the light, searching for a trace of the blue. The protection has left little to see.

Three heartbeats of feeling cold. Three heartbeats of seeing blue. Three heartbeats of being human.

The path continues upward into the mountains. She has yet to name the destination. It is a direction she feels, leading into the cold above her. The work continues along it.

Xiǎohú stands on a rock farther up the trail. She looks back as Yun lowers her hands. When Yun takes another step, the fox turns uphill again.

Yun follows. Wind moves her clothes, but its cold lies outside the protection again. She remembers the sensation against her fingertips clearly enough to tell what has changed.

But it had penetrated.

Above the ridge, the clouds hang heavy and gray.

She climbs.
