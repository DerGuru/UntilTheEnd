Yun sits on the stone floor with her hands open on her knees. Her palms face upward. She has let her fingers rest where they fell, too tired to arrange them into another posture of intention.

The cave is dark. Outside, stars would give shape to the sky and a horizon would separate it from the land. Here she sees neither the floor under her nor the wall she knows is near.

She sits in the dark because the dark is where she is.

A sound rises under the dripping. Stone settles somewhere deeper, a low click followed by a shift that trembles faintly through the floor. The mountain moves so little that she would miss it in daylight. Here it is clear.

She breathes into the cold. The air leaves her mouth, though the dark hides its cloud.

The fear is still there.

She examines the fear from the inside. In places it is smooth, worn by the attention she has given it without knowing. Other parts stay rough. She finds an abrupt edge where a thought meets it, where an ordinary breath tightens. She studies those differences until she can recognize them.

The fear has volume. It occupies her chest like a presence, displaces other things, and takes up room.

She sits with it.

She sits with it as she sits with the cave's cold. It does not shrink under any opinion she forms. For now she learns the space it occupies and does not try to force it out.

A cold gust enters the cave mouth. Ash lifts from the remains of the fire and settles, faintly audible, before the air stills.

Yesterday she could explain the fear. Today she follows it as it changes her breathing. An emotion that has lived in her for millennia has a shape now. She knows where she meets it, even when she can do nothing about it.

The form offers little relief, but it changes how she approaches the feeling. A map cannot make difficult ground passable. It can show where the ground lies. She has a place now from which to consider the next step. What that step will be, she does not yet know.

Yun stands.

She walks toward the cave mouth. Morning light reaches the entrance, diffuse gray under an overcast sky. The wall becomes visible first, then the rough floor before her feet.

Small ridges press against her soles as she crosses it, each step a surface she can tell apart.

She stops at the mouth. The mountain lies under the clouds as it did before she entered. Her eyes follow the slope, the ground she will have to cross.

The wind touches her face. Cold. Real. Felt.

Behind her lies the dark where she found the fear. Ahead is the ground on which she will have to live with it. She waits at the threshold for one more breath.

Xiǎohú emerges at her side, blinking amber eyes in the gray light. She shakes herself and looks up at Yun.

She stands. She breathes. The air tastes of rain.

Nobody tells her what to do next.

She has to decide that on her own. 

Like it's always been. 

Yun takes a step.
