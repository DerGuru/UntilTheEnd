Above the treeline, the qi-column pushed the moisture apart. The sky tore. The column had hit a density threshold that the atmosphere couldn't absorb, scattering the thin veil of water vapor overhead. A hole opened in that veil. Circular, expanding, the edges defined and sharp like a wound in fabric. Through it: sky that was too blue, light that was too bright, the glare of a high-altitude vantage point reaching the ground without the softening haze. Without water, dust, and the ten thousand small mercies that atmosphere provides.

Xu Ran was in the column.

Normal vision couldn't penetrate this density of saturated air. At this level everything reads to me as signature and pattern, and he was a structure of qi so densely organized that it had its own geometry. He was the center of the spiral, the axis around which everything else rotated. His body was barely visible. The qi around him was too bright, too concentrated, the energy radiating from his core washing out the flesh inside it. He was a shape inside light. A pattern inside noise.

He was screaming.

The sound had passed beyond human. It had started as a scream, the involuntary vocalization of a body being rebuilt down to its smallest parts, flesh destroyed and recreated as the cultivator's foundation reconstituted itself around the new core. But the qi had caught the vibration, amplified it, harmonized it, and turned it into something that operated below the frequency of human hearing while rising above it. A sound that existed in the chest rather than the ears.

The ground shook. Continuously now, a sustained vibration, the earth itself resonating with the column's frequency. The glazed surface under my feet hummed. My bones hummed. Everything hummed.

This time I stood fifty paces away, inside the blast radius of a Core Formation tribulation, with nothing between me and the expanding energy except thousands of years of existence that made standing here roughly equivalent to standing in moderate wind.

The tribulation was, from a technical standpoint, impressive. Xu Ran's control was exceptional. The spiral maintained its structure throughout the expansion phase, each layer unfolding in sequence rather than detonating simultaneously. This was discipline, the product of methodical preparation, of consolidation that had compressed the energy into a structure built for maximum efficiency and minimum waste.

From a different standpoint, that of the valley, the village, and the fifty-four people on the southern slope, it was a natural disaster wearing a young man's face.

The column brightened. The spiral accelerated. The threshold approached.

He was going through.

It came like a wave gathering before it breaks. The energy built, compressed, and reached the point where containment equals capacity. Whatever holds it either integrates or detonates. Everything that follows depended on Xu Ran's dantian.

Integration.

He was going to make it. The structure was sound. The layers held. The core was forming, the energy folding inward, the seal closing. The new foundation crystallized into something permanent, powerful, and fundamentally altered from what had existed before.

The shockwave would come at the moment of completion. The excess energy, everything the spiral had gathered that the new core couldn't contain, would be expelled outward in a second massive discharge. The physics were non-negotiable. You couldn't forge a core without waste. The waste had to go somewhere.

Somewhere was here. And everywhere for seven li in every direction.

The spiral tightened. The last rotation. The final compression.

The moment of completion.
