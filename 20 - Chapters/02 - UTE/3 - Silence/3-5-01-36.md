I stood alone outside in the different air. Wei was still in bed. The destabilization was repaired and the air was clear again, but something else was in it: the pressure of trained beings occupying space with the intention of not being noticed. Their effort made a signature louder than silence would have been, to anyone who could hear.

Three qi-signatures at the tree line, northeast.

They were dampened, disciplined, and professional, their qi compressed by training. The suppression worked against most perception. Mine reached them easily.

They stood in a triangle, the arrangement of three-point surveillance. Each position's coverage overlapped with its neighbor's, leaving a continuous field of observation with no gaps. Textbook. I had helped write it, in a manner of speaking, several lifetimes ago. These people worked as a unit, with the discipline of professionals rather than curious individuals.

Iron Lotus. I recognized the doctrine of positioning, which I had been cataloguing since Fang Liang. It now showed up as scouts, organized and determined.

These were no longer recruiters. Recruiters worked alone, approached openly, and smiled. These cultivators held their distance and observed. They were gathering intelligence instead of making offers.

Hunters was the more accurate word. They had gone from wanting Wei to planning his acquisition, the difference between desire and strategy. Strategy produced triangle formations at tree lines before dawn.

I drank my tea and didn't look toward the tree line. I didn't need my eyes. The qi-sensing that was my oldest function required neither line of sight nor attention, only existence. And I existed, standing in a doorway with cold tea, aware of three professionals watching a settlement that contained a boy who was the whole purpose of the watching.

I catalogued.

Position one: northeast, forty paces from the tree line. Male, Foundation stage, mid-twenties. His suppression was competent but leaked at the lower frequencies, the bass note of his qi-signature humming under the dampening like a heartbeat through a wall.

Position two: east-northeast, sixty paces. Female, possibly Inner Disciple level. Her suppression was better, her leakage minimal, professional-grade. She had been doing this longer than position one.

Position three: north, thirty-five paces. Unknown level. Unknown gender. The suppression was exquisite. Nothing leaked. This person was either very skilled at concealment or very weak. But the very weak didn't deploy in triangle formations, so very skilled it was.

Three hunters watching, mapping, and preparing.

For what?

The answer was behind me, sleeping. The boy whose qi-signature had been transmitted on jade and had produced the triangle at the tree line.

They wouldn't act today. Scouts acted when scouting was complete, and that took time. Days, probably. Maybe seven. The patience of an authorized operation. Hierarchies moved at the speed of gatherings, and gatherings were not fast. Nothing in all my years had disabused me of that.

But they would act soon.

I drank my cold tea. The sun rose. The garden's three pots were recovering. The settlement began its day.

I said nothing. To no one.

The air tasted as it always did. I had tea, three hunters, and a sleeping boy. The distance between these things was measured in paces, and the paces were insufficient.
