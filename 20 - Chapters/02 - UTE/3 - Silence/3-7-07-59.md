The question remained.

Unanswered, and that was the point. Maybe forever. Maybe it was not answerable, and that was the larger point. It belonged to the category of questions designed not for answers but for carrying.

It would drive me into the investigation it demanded, an investigation of myself rather than of the world, of qi, or of the architecture of cultivation. Of whatever I was. Of the protection that was mine and was not his. The crime. The accusation directed at me, at whatever part of me was the mechanism, whatever part was the dao or the curse or the fundamental condition that selected for my survival and against his.

But not today.

Today I stood in the forest with a question.

The wind moved through the trees. The branches shifted. The forest creaked and rustled, the small sounds that constituted the background, the world's way of saying that it was here, and not listening, but here.

I stood.

The brown circle held steady at three paces, neither growing nor shrinking, the equilibrium of an emission tied to something that had reached its floor. This level. This radius. This amount of dying.

I would carry the question into whatever came after this forest, after this rain, after this gray series of days that constituted the aftermath of a death that had no parallel. Carrying was the only verb available.

Whatever protects me, why not him?

The answer existed. It had to exist, somewhere in the architecture of whatever I was and whatever it was that kept me alive and unharmed. The answer was findable.

But not today.

Today the question stood alone, without answer, in a forest, in rain that had stopped, in the brown circle of a woman whose grief killed grass and whose question killed something else: the comfort of not-knowing, the peace of assumption, the safety of believing that the universe's indifference was general rather than targeted.

The protection was targeted. At me. But the targeting excluded everyone else.

And the exclusion was the thing I would dismantle.

But not today.
