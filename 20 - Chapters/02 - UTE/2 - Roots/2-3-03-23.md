We'd descended into a warmer, greener valley, where a cold river ran between the ranges. It was wide enough for Wei to stop on the bank to look across. The shallow water ran clear over the stones. We could cross here before making the next climb.

First, Wei meditated on the bank. He had followed the sequence I'd prescribed every morning for many days now, settling into it without a reminder.

He sat cross-legged with his eyes closed, breathing three and three.

Then the click.

Ten paces away, my qi-perception registered the sharp shift. His qi compressed, drew together, and reshaped the flow through his channels in a single contraction.

Foundation Establishment, middle phase. He had taken nine days to advance from early phase. Usually that took three to six months.

He opened bright eyes and grinned at me. He held the grin, waiting until he was certain I was looking.

"I'm getting better."

He leaped up, strengthening his steps with qi. He crossed faster than before, barely touching the water. Two bounds took him to the opposite bank. He landed and laughed.

It was his first full laugh since the village, and it carried over the running water.

"Did you see that? Two steps!"

"I saw."

"Two STEPS, Yun. Across a river."

"I know."

He bounded back, still grinning. One foot landed in the shallows. Water sloshed over his ankle.

"Hm."

Water drained from his trouser leg while I checked his channels again. Growth this fast could end in a sudden rupture.

He bounded back and forth across the river, grinning each time he landed.

I held still on the bank while his qi surged.
