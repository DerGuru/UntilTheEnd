On the third night in the mountains, Wei fell asleep almost as soon as he lay down. He'd trained hard, spending a watch and a half on compression exercises, then another watch walking. The vibration started while he slept.

I heard it before I felt it.

A hum. Below sound, below the frequency human ears registered, a vibration that traveled through ground rather than air.

His core.

I extended my senses. Wei's core was vibrating. Steady. Rhythmic. A second heartbeat, slower than his biological one, operating in a frequency range that had no precedent. Nothing in all my years of observation.

New. And "new" in cultivation was either innovation or malfunction. The difference was only clear retrospectively.

His face was tense, even in sleep. Jaw clenched. Breathing regular but labored.

And then his face began to glow.

Literally. I need to be precise: Wei's face, in the firelight, began to emit light.

Faintly. A warm gold shone from within him, through skin, bone, and the qi-structures interpenetrating all of it. His qi was surfacing, physically manifesting. Qi became visible only at extreme densities or extreme instabilities. Both were extreme in him, so the manifestation was logical. Terrifying and logical.

I stood, moved closer, and knelt. All those years. Now I knelt by a sleeping boy, watching his face produce light from the inside: warm, beautiful, and wrong.

His hair shone too. Individual strands were luminous, each one lit along its length in the dark.

I looked at the ground.

His shadow.

Wei's body lay between the fire and the ground. It should have thrown a shadow. Solid things blocked light.

He had no shadow.

His own light overpowered the fire. He was brighter, at his surface, than the flames. The shadow that should have confirmed his body as something solid, opaque, and capable of blocking light was absent.

He cast no shadow.

I stayed there, looking at the ground where his shadow should have been. The fire crackled. The mountains rose dark above us. A thirteen-year-old boy glowed in his sleep, shadowless, his core humming at a frequency that had no precedent.

In sleep, when he stopped consciously holding it in, this was Wei. A signal. A lighthouse lit from within by a fuel source that didn't diminish.

The glow faded slowly over many breaths. The luminescence retreated through the skin, back to the core.

His shadow returned, faint in the lower firelight. But present. A dark shape on stone, confirming he was solid. That he was there.

I sat down. Across the fire. In my place.

He murmured a word in his sleep. I didn't catch it. The ordinary sound of a boy dreaming.

I watched. Until dawn.
