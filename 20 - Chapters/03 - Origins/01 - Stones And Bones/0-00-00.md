You know how her story ends.

You've seen the weight of it — the tiredness, the silence, the thing inside her that would not let go. You've watched her find a reason to stay. You've watched her find her peace. Finally.

But there was a beginning.

Before Yun. Before the cultivation world had words for what she was. Before immortality became a prison she couldn't escape.

There was a girl. Eight summers old. A tribe that moved with the water. A friend who climbed trees faster than anyone. A warmth under the earth that nobody else could feel.

She didn't know what she was building. She didn't know it couldn't be undone.
