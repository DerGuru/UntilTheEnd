By the end of that stretch she could stand in the middle of the largest thing people had ever built and feel nothing at all.

There was a city, one of the nameless ones, because by then they blurred together into the same city getting bigger. She moved through it during a festival, at the height of it. The streets were packed shoulder to shoulder. Music in three keys at once, smoke off a hundred cook-fires, painted faces, and a whole people spending itself on being alive for one loud night. She walked through the middle of it like weather through a street. She could see that it was joy. She could not feel that it was.

People think the worst loneliness is being far from others. It is not. She had spent whole empty centuries in mountains, and they were nothing beside that crowd. Alone in the mountains was only the truth of her situation, and it sat easy. Alone in the crowd was a thing she had to choose, breath by breath, against ten thousand invitations not to be. There is no loneliness like continuing after you have lost the knack of joining.

And still, her hands did the small good things. That is the part I hold onto, when I am trying to be fair to what she had become. In that same festival crowd a child went down under the feet of it, as children do. She had it up, out, and set on a wall before she had decided anything at all. Old habit. Her hands still knew the shape of help even when the rest of her had stopped meaning it. The mother wept her thanks. Mei felt the gratitude land on her and slide off. She handed the child across, and had forgotten them both by the next corner. But she had done it. Some worn thread in her still pulled toward it, without heat, without choosing. She did not know then how much would one day hang on that thread.

That was what she had become by the time the world got around to wanting her. Not cruel. Not raging. Empty in a smooth, functional, portable fashion, doing good out of reflex and feeling none of it, watching everything, needing nothing. A woman like that is not at peace. A woman like that is only available. She had no reason to do anything and no reason not to. A thing with no reasons left will do whatever the first strong hand asks of it. She did not know she was waiting. But she was ready, and ready was enough.
