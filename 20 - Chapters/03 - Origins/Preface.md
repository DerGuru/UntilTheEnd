# Origins

Origins is a collection of standalone books. Each one marks a milestone on Yun's path through time — a moment that shaped her, broke her, or refused to let her stop.

*Stones and Bones* is the first. Others will follow. Eventually.

Each book will be self-contained. No cliffhangers, no required reading order beyond the first. Just points of light across a very long darkness.

I'll write them as the ideas come. If you have one — a moment in her history you'd like to see — feel free to reach out.

The fiction is marked as complete because the current state is complete. When a new book enters the workshop, the status will change accordingly.
