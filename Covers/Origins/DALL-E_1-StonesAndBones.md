# DALL-E Prompt — Stones and Bones Cover

## Prompt (direkt in ChatGPT einfügen)

```
Create a portrait-format book cover illustration (5:8 aspect ratio) for a prehistoric fantasy novel.

The scene: A feral teenage girl (15-16 years old) stands on a high mountain ridge at pre-dawn, seen from a low three-quarter back angle. She is lean and angular with scarred bare arms, long wind-tangled dark hair, and wears worn animal hides that have been patched many times. She is barefoot on cold grey stone. She holds a crude stone-tipped spear low at her side — not raised, just carried. Her posture radiates predatory stillness: weight forward, shoulders set, chin lifted, surveying the vast prehistoric wilderness below.

Far below in the valley, three thin columns of campfire smoke rise — tiny and distant, the only sign of human life in an endless landscape of rolling grassland, dark forest patches, and a river barely catching first light. No roads, no structures, no civilization anywhere.

The lighting is pre-dawn: the sky graduates from deep slate-grey at the top to a thin muted ochre line at the horizon. Not golden, not warm — just the first pale suggestion of light beginning. A faint bone-white rim-light catches the edge of her silhouette (shoulder, hair, spear tip). The rest of her remains in cool shadow. There is a very subtle heat-distortion shimmer around her hands and forearms, like air above cooling embers — barely visible.

Behind her, half-hidden by rock: a dark cave entrance. Her den.

The ridge itself is raw grey stone with lichen patches and wind-bent scrub. The entire color palette is dominated by cool slate-grey and muted earth tones. Warmth is sparse, distant, almost absent.

Composition for book cover use: The top 30% of the image should be a calm, clean dark sky gradient (empty space for a title to be added later). The bottom 12% should be slightly darker stone (space for author name). The girl's silhouette should read clearly even at thumbnail size.

Style: Painterly realism with cinematic lighting. Desaturated, muted color palette. Atmospheric depth. The mood is emotionally quiet, raw, and primal. Do not include any text, logos, watermarks, or typography in the image.
```

## Variante (nähere Einstellung)

```
Create a portrait-format book cover illustration (5:8 aspect ratio) for a prehistoric fantasy novel. Close three-quarter back view.

A feral teenage girl stands on a mountain ridge in pre-dawn darkness. She is 15-16, lean and angular with scarred arms, long tangled dark hair blowing sideways in wind, wearing simple worn animal hides. She holds a stone-tipped spear low at her side, gazing down at distant campfire smoke in a vast prehistoric valley below — rolling grassland, dark forests, a river glinting faintly. No civilization visible anywhere.

The sky is deep slate above, with only a thin muted ochre line at the horizon — pale, not golden. A faint bone-white rim-light outlines her silhouette edge. There is a very subtle heat-shimmer around her hands, almost invisible. The ridge is cool grey stone with lichen. A dark cave mouth is partially visible behind her.

Style: Painterly realism, desaturated muted palette, cinematic lighting, atmospheric depth. Emotionally quiet, raw, primal.

Composition: Top area should have clean dark gradient (space for title text to be added later). Bottom area slightly darker (space for author name). Strong readable silhouette even at small size. Do not include any text, logos, or watermarks.
```

## Hinweise für ChatGPT/DALL-E

- Seitenverhältnis direkt angeben: "portrait-format, 5:8 aspect ratio" oder alternativ "tall portrait, 1024x1792"
- Negativanweisungen als natürliche Sätze: "Do not include text, logos, watermarks, neon colors, modern elements, magic effects, or multiple characters"
- Falls zu bunt: "Use a strictly desaturated palette. No saturated colors. Cool greys dominate." hinzufügen
- Falls Gesicht statt Rücken: "The viewer sees her from behind. Her face is not visible." betonen
- Falls zu viel Fantasy-Kitsch: "This is prehistoric, not medieval. No metal, no armor, no swords, no magic glowing effects." ergänzen
- Falls Heat-Shimmer nicht sichtbar: Kann weggelassen werden — ist ein subtiles Detail
- Typografie wird separat in Canva/Photoshop hinzugefügt
