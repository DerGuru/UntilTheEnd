# DALL-E Prompt — Origins Series Cover

## Prompt (direkt in ChatGPT einfügen)

```
Create a portrait-format book cover illustration (5:8 aspect ratio) for a fantasy series about a journey through prehistoric to ancient epochs.

The scene: A single narrow path winds from the foreground upward through a vast landscape that transitions through different historical eras, like geological strata made visible. The image reads from bottom to top as a journey through time.

Bottom foreground: Raw grassland and brown earth in muted warm tones. A hand axe and a bone needle rest on the ground beside the path. This is the world at its beginning — stone age, primal, simple.

Middle section: The path winds upward through a transitioning landscape. On the left side, faint hints of bronze blades and a half-shadowed campfire. On the right, an iron-age wooden post and a weathered shield at the roadside. The transitions between eras are flowing and misty — one age bleeding into the next like fog pulling time forward. No hard borders.

Upper background: Mountains, forest, and at the far horizon a barely-visible silhouette of curved rooftops and a single distant tower in haze — an East Asian fantasy world suggested but never clearly shown. Where the journey leads, eventually.

On the path: A single woman, small against the landscape, seen from behind. Walking. Her silhouette is deliberately ambiguous — her clothing could be furs, simple robes, or a plain cloak. No detail, only form. She is upright, watchful, with the posture of someone who has been walking for a very long time and does not intend to stop. In one hand she carries a weapon that is not clearly identifiable as spear or sword. Something sharp. Something carried.

Subtle details: A single ember-orange point of light glows at the path's edge in the foreground — not fire, just a coal that refuses to die. A shallow pool of water with a mirror-still surface shows the reflection of a younger figure — a child where a woman walks above. The faint shadow of a large animal at the path's edge, half in mist, neither threatening nor friendly.

Color palette: Warm muted browns at the bottom graduating to cool indigo-blue at the top. The transitions between epochs carry subtle misty layering. The ember is the warmest accent in the image. The future (top) is cooler, further away, more complex.

Composition: The top 30% should be a calm dark sky gradient, clean empty space for a title to be added later. The bottom 12% should be slightly darker earth, space for an author name. The overall composition should be readable even at thumbnail size.

Style: Painterly realism with cinematic lighting and atmospheric depth. Emotionally quiet. Epic in scale but intimate in tone. Do not include any text, logos, watermarks, or typography.
```

## Kompakte Variante

```
Create a tall portrait book cover illustration (5:8 ratio) for a fantasy series.

A narrow path winds from warm brown foreground up into deep indigo twilight sky. The landscape transitions through layered historical epochs like geological strata: the bottom is raw prehistoric grassland with stone tools on the ground, the middle transitions through bronze and iron age hints in flowing mist, and the top shows a barely-visible distant East Asian cityscape silhouette on the horizon.

A single woman walks the path, seen from behind, small against the vast landscape. Her silhouette is ambiguous — could be wearing furs or robes or a cloak. She carries a weapon. Upright, relentless, alone.

Details: An ember glows orange at the path's edge. A still pool of water reflects a younger version of her — a child where a woman walks. A faint shadow of a large animal lurks in the mist at the roadside.

Color: Warm muted bottom graduating to cool indigo top. Painterly realism, cinematic lighting, atmospheric depth. Top area clean dark for title space, bottom darker for author name space. Do not include any text, logos, or watermarks.
```

## Hinweise für ChatGPT/DALL-E

- Seitenverhältnis: "portrait-format, 5:8 aspect ratio" oder "tall portrait, 1024x1792"
- Kern-Motiv: Wanderschaft durch Zeit. Beharrlichkeit, nicht Heldentum.
- Schlüsseldetail: Die Spiegelung (Frau oben / Kind im Wasser) — wenn nicht umgesetzt, explizit nachfordern
- Falls Epochen zu scharf getrennt: "The transitions between eras should be seamless and misty, with no hard borders. One age flows into the next like fog." hinzufügen
- Falls Figur zu groß: "The woman should be tiny against the vast landscape. Extreme sense of scale." ergänzen
- Falls zu bunt: "Strictly muted, desaturated palette. No saturated or vibrant colors." hinzufügen
- Falls xianxia-Silhouette zu deutlich: "The distant buildings should be barely visible, more suggestion than detail, almost lost in haze."
- Typografie wird separat in Canva/Photoshop hinzugefügt
