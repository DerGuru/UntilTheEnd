# Midjourney Prompt — Stones and Bones Cover

## Prompt

```
A feral teenage east asian girl crouches on a high mountain ridge at dawn, seen from a low three-quarter back angle. She is 15-16, lean, angular, scarred bare arms, long wind-tangled dark hair, wearing worn animal hides patched many times. Barefoot on cold grey stone. She holds a crude stone-tipped spear low at her side, not raised, just carried. Her posture is predatory stillness — weight forward, shoulders set, chin lifted, surveying the vast prehistoric wilderness below.

Far below in the valley, three thin columns of campfire smoke rise — tiny, distant, the only sign of human life in an endless landscape of rolling grassland, dark forest patches, and a river catching first light. No roads, no structures, no civilization.

Dawn barely breaking behind eastern mountains. Sky graduates from deep slate at top to muted ochre at horizon — not golden, not warm, just the first pale suggestion of light. Faint rim-light catches her silhouette edge — thin, desaturated, more bone-white than gold. Rest of her in cool shadow. Subtle heat-distortion shimmer around her hands and forearms, like air above cooling embers.

Behind her, half-hidden by rock: dark cave entrance. Her den.

The ridge: raw grey stone, lichen patches, wind-bent scrub. Color palette dominated by cool slate-grey and muted earth tones. Warmth is sparse, distant, almost absent — a single pale ochre line at the horizon. Cold world with no comfort in it.

Top 30% of image is calm dark sky gradient, clean for title text. Bottom 12% is darker stone, calm for author name. Strong readable silhouette at thumbnail size.

Painterly realism, restrained anime sensibility, cinematic lighting, desaturated muted palette, atmospheric depth, emotionally quiet, raw, primal --ar 5:8 --s 750 --style raw
```

## Hinweise

- `--ar 5:8` für Buchcover-Proportionen (alternativ `--ar 2:3`)
- `--s 750` für hohe Stilisierung
- `--style raw` für weniger MJ-Eigengeschmack, mehr Prompt-Treue
- Text/Typografie NICHT im Prompt — wird in Canva/Photoshop hinzugefügt
- Bei zu viel Anime: `--style raw` beibehalten, "anime" aus dem Prompt entfernen
- Bei zu wenig Wildheit: "feral, predatory, dangerous" verstärken
- Wenn MJ Gesicht zeigt statt Rücken: "seen from behind, back to viewer" explizit hinzufügen
