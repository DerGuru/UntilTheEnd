# Until the End — Back Cover Blurbs

---

## Book 1: Embers

She is older than civilization. More powerful than gods. And she wants nothing more than to die.

For millennia, Yun has wandered alone — untouchable, unkillable, and profoundly tired. Every attempt to end her existence fails. Every connection she forms turns to ash. She has stopped trying.

Then a twelve-year-old farm boy follows her into the wilderness and refuses to leave.

Wei is loud, stubborn, and completely unimpressed by the most dangerous being in existence. He doesn't want her power. He doesn't fear her. He just won't go away.

For the first time in thousands of years, something stirs. Not hope — she's too old for that. But something close enough to keep her walking.

*A cultivation fantasy about immortality, loneliness, and the stubborn persistence of a child who doesn't know when to quit.*

---

## Book 2: Roots

The world has noticed her.

Yun's existence was a secret kept by sheer indifference. Now that she's revealed herself, sects mobilize, cultivators scheme, and hunters close in — not for her life, but for the impossible power she represents.

Running should be simple. She's done it for ages. But Wei is growing — faster than any cultivator should. His body burns with borrowed strength, and the source is something neither of them can control.

The further they travel, the clearer the price becomes: everything that makes Wei extraordinary is killing him.

And Yun cannot stop it.

*The journey continues. The world pushes back. And the boy who made her stay is becoming something that cannot last.*

---

## Book 3: Silence

Everything Yun feared is coming true.

Wei is fourteen now — stronger, faster, more reckless. He fights like someone who believes he's invincible. And in a world of cultivators and sects, that confidence draws exactly the kind of attention they've been running from.

Yun has kept him alive through cunning, through power, through sheer refusal to let the world take what's hers. But Wei is no longer a boy who hides behind her. He steps forward. He protects others.

And some battles have a price that even she cannot pay.

*The third volume of Until the End. Some promises can't be kept.*

---

## Book 4: Echoes

She keeps walking.

Not because she wants to. Not because there's anywhere to go. But because her body won't stop, her lungs won't quit, and the world keeps turning whether she participates or not.

Xu Ran — scholar and chronicler now — walks beside her. He talks. She doesn't answer. He asks questions. She forgets he exists.

But something is changing. The world around her bends in ways it shouldn't. Reality stutters. And the question that has haunted her for millennia shifts:

Not *why can't I die?*

But *what is keeping me alive?*

*Grief doesn't end. But sometimes, in the silence after loss, you hear the first real question.*

---

## Book 5: Fractures

She is close to the answer. And something inside her is terrified of it.

Every time Yun reaches for the truth, her mind goes blank. Memories dissolve mid-thought. Reality warps around her — not gently, not subtly. Mountains vanish. Rivers reverse. The world cracks along fault lines that follow her footsteps.

Her own subconscious is fighting her. Whatever keeps her immortal does not want to be found.

The search is destroying everything around her. The people she travels with. The landscape itself. And still she pushes — because the alternative is eternity.

*The cage has a lock. The lock has a key. The key is in the hand of the prisoner. And the prisoner cannot look down.*

---

## Book 6: Mirrors

The wall inside her mind is failing.

Something has protected Yun from herself — erasing memories, blocking realizations, deleting the truth every time she reached it. Now the cracks are too wide. The images come faster. Clearer. A woman who looks like her, doing things that should be impossible. Xu Ran's notes filling page after page with evidence she can no longer dismiss.

She can feel it — the shape of the answer, just beyond reach. Every test brings her closer. Every anomaly confirms what she suspects but cannot hold. The world bends around her in ways that defy cultivation, defy physics, defy everything she thought she understood about power.

The sect arrives at its peak — fused, boosted, dangerous in a way they never were before. For Yun, they are not the threat. They never were. The threat is what she'll find when the last wall falls.

And it will fall.

Xu Ran said it best, sitting by a fire, his notebook open: "You're not afraid of the answer. You're afraid of what comes after."

He was right. He usually is. She hates that about him.

*Knowing the truth is not the same as changing it. The hardest battle of the series begins.*

---

## Book 7: Clouds

She knows what she is. She knows what she must do. She knows it will be the hardest thing she has ever attempted.

Not fight. Not endure. Not survive.

Let go.

Ten thousand years of absolute conviction that she is immortal — woven into every cell, every breath, every unconscious thought. To become mortal, she doesn't need to break the belief. She needs to stop holding it.

There are no enemies left. No external threats. No dramatic confrontations.

Just a woman in a cave, learning to die.

*The final volume of Until the End. It ends in silence — the kind that comes after a long breath, finally released.*

## Series Blurb

She was born before language had a word for loneliness.

Ten thousand years of cultivation have made Yun the most powerful being in existence — and the most tired. She has outlived civilizations, buried everyone she ever loved, and tried every method imaginable to end her own life. Nothing works. Something keeps her alive, and she has long since stopped caring what.

Then a twelve-year-old farm boy refuses to leave her alone.

Wei doesn't understand what she is. He doesn't care. He's loud, stubborn, and unshakeable — and for the first time in millennia, Yun finds herself walking forward instead of waiting to stop.

But immortality has rules she never agreed to. The closer she holds something, the faster it slips away. And the force keeping her alive may not be what she thinks it is.

*Until the End* is a seven-book cultivation fantasy series about immortality, grief, and the terrifying possibility that the cage was never locked from the outside. Complete at over 400 chapters.

**For readers of:** Frieren, The Man From Earth, One Punch Man, Dead Tired

## About the Author

Andreas Jakof was born in 1978 in East Germany. He was twelve when the Wall came down. He studied business, although in his heart, he is a software developer by passion. He followed his wife across the country until they settled near Berlin, where they live with their daughter.

*Until the End* is his first series — written in three months, fueled by coffee and the curiosity, whether he could make it, and proof that sometimes the best stories come from people who were supposed to be doing something else.
