# Until The End: Origins — Synopsis, Genres & Tags

## Synopsis (Serie)

**Short (Tagline):**
She was the first to feel the world breathe. It never let her stop.

**Medium (Blurb — ~150 Wörter):**
Before there were cultivators, before there were sects or scriptures or names for what flows through the world — there was a girl who felt something no one else could feel.

She called it the Warm.

In a Stone Age tribe where survival is measured in seasons and the strongest man rules by size alone, Mei discovers she can sense things beneath the earth, inside living things, in the space between heartbeats. She hides it. For three years. Then she uses it to protect the only person who matters, and her tribe casts her out.

Alone in the wilderness, she becomes something that has never existed before — not through training or teachings, but through the desperate logic of a child who refuses to die. She forges a path with no name. She carries a purpose she cannot articulate. And every time the world tries to end her, she answers with two words:

*Not now.*

This is her origin. All hundred thousand years of it.

**Long (Full Synopsis — ~300 Wörter):**
Until The End: Origins follows a single woman across millennia — from the first flicker of cultivation in a prehistoric tribe to the threshold of the xianxia world readers know from Until The End.

She has carried many names. The first was Mei.

At eight, Mei senses warmth beneath the earth and a hum inside living things. No one else feels it. She hides what she can do, but at eleven, when the tribe's leader threatens her closest friend, instinct overrides caution. An uncontrolled burst of force sends the strongest man in the tribe to the ground. Everyone sees. No one forgives. She is exiled.

Alone in the mountains, Mei learns to hunt, to gather what she later won't call Qi, and to survive winters that should kill her. In a cave pulsing with the Warm, she accidentally creates something permanent — a Dao of Continuation, the seed of immortality — without knowing what she's done or that it can never be undone.

When a spear pierces her chest and the wound closes before the blood dries, the world changes. Not for her. Because of her.

Each book in the Origins series covers a different era of her journey: the Stone Age, the Bronze Age, the Iron Age, and beyond — toward the cultivation world of sects, spirit beasts, and tribulations. She will fight wars, forge weapons, lose companions, gain names, and walk alone through centuries of silence. The constant: an ember in her chest that will not go out, and the quiet refusal that keeps her moving.

She did not choose immortality. She chose *not yet*.

The rest followed.

## What to expect

A **prehistoric origin story** for a xianxia world — cultivation before the word existed.

**Earned progression** over millennia: no systems, no shortcuts, no chosen-one mechanics. Every gain costs something real.

A **female lead** who is powerful, alone, and never pitied — written close, not soft.

**Grounded survival** first, supernatural power second. She hunts before she cultivates.

A **calm, visceral tone** with spare dialogue, dry restraint, and violence that lands because it's rare.

**Episodic structure**: each book covers a different era of her life — Stone Age, Bronze Age, Iron Age, and beyond — readable as standalones.

The long, quiet answer to how the woman in *Until The End* became what she is.
